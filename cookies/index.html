Cookie Policy

Effective Date: December 10, 2025

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit FormBridge. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site. Cookies allow us to recognize your device and store information about your preferences or past actions.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to ensure that FormBridge functions correctly, to prevent fraud, and to improve the academic user experience. We do not use cookies to track your browsing habits across third-party websites for advertising purposes.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the operation of the platform. They enable you to log into secure areas of the university portal, use e-billing services, and submit forms. Without these cookies, the services you have asked for cannot be provided.

  • Session Cookies: Maintained only while you are navigating the site to keep you logged in.
  • Security Cookies: Used to detect authentication abuses and prevent Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) attacks.

Functionality Cookies

These allow the platform to remember choices you make to provide a more personalized experience. This includes remembering your preferred language, text size, or the specific university department view you selected.

Performance and Analytics Cookies

We use these to collect information about how visitors use FormBridge, for instance, which pages users go to most often and if they get error messages from web pages. All information these cookies collect is aggregated and therefore anonymous. It is only used to improve how the website works.

4. Third-Party Cookies

In some cases, we may use trusted third-party services (such as Google Analytics or university authentication gateways) that may place cookies on your device. We have no control over these third-party cookies. Please review the privacy policies of these third-party services for more information.

5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Most web browsers allow some control of most cookies through the browser settings. You can choose to block all cookies, accept only first-party cookies, or delete cookies upon closing your browser.

Note: If you disable strictly necessary cookies, some parts of FormBridge (such as form submission and account login) will not function properly.

6. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time in order to reflect, for example, changes to the cookies we use or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Please re-visit this Cookie Policy regularly to stay informed about our use of cookies and related technologies.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, please contact the Data Protection Officer at lutancorpinfoteam@gmail.com
