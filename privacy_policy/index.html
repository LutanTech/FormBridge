Privacy Policy

How FormBridge collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Introduction

FormBridge provides digital tools that help university students and administrators manage forms faster and more efficiently. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, stored, used, and protected when you use our platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that helps us deliver and improve our services. This includes:

  • Account Information: Your name, email, phone number, and password.
  • Form Submission Data: Any information and files you submit through forms.
  • Device and Usage Data: Browser type, device type, IP address, and pages visited.
  • Cookies: Used to improve your experience and remember preferences.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Manage your account and verify your identity.
  • Process and store form submissions securely.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve system performance and user experience.
  • Prevent fraud and maintain system security.

4. How We Store and Protect Your Data

All data is stored securely and is only accessible to authorized personnel. We use encryption, secure servers, and strict access controls to protect your information.

However, no online service can guarantee 100 percent security, so we encourage users to keep their passwords safe.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties. We may share limited information only when:

  • Required by law or legal obligation.
  • Necessary to protect the safety or rights of users or the platform.
  • Working with trusted service providers who help us operate the system under strict confidentiality agreements.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

FormBridge uses cookies to remember user preferences, improve performance, and analyze website traffic. You can control cookie settings from your browser.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.

8. Children’s Privacy

FormBridge is intended for university-level users. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When changes are made, the updated version will be posted on this page.

10. Contact Information

If you have questions or need help regarding your privacy, contact us at: lutancorpinfoteam@gmail.com.
